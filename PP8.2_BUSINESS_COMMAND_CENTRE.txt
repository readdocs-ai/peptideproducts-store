PP8.2 BUSINESS COMMAND CENTRE

Built from the exact PP8.1-MOBILE-FIRST-MASTER project supplied by the user.

KEY UPDATES
- New /admin executive dashboard with today, 7-day, monthly and overall KPIs.
- 14-day sales chart, fulfilment summary, recent paid orders and top products.
- New /admin/analytics with 30-day and 6-month trends, product performance and country sales.
- New /admin/inventory read-only catalogue and sales-performance overview.
- Printable packing slip/order record at /admin/orders/[order-id]/print.
- Existing Bank & Accountant Centre preserved.
- Existing Customer Database preserved.
- Redis optimisation: order lists now use LRANGE + MGET instead of KEYS plus per-order GETs.
- Direct Stripe-session and SumUp checkout lookup keys are created for new/updated orders.
- Historical sessions retain a lightweight compatibility fallback.
- Admin live-order refresh reduced from every 30 seconds to every 5 minutes.

IMPORTANT FINANCE NOTE
Exact Stripe processing fees and true profit are not stored in the current order database, so PP8.2 does not invent or estimate those figures. Bank reports continue to show recorded order totals, refunds and adjustments accurately.
