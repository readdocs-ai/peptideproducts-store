PP10.2 emergency pricing fix

- Removed the automatic Buy 2 Get 1 Free promotion from cart, checkout and server-side Stripe checkout.
- New orders now charge the exact product subtotal plus configured shipping (UK shipping currently free).

- Historical orders that already received the old automatic promotion are not altered. Admin screens, print view and order emails now show the inferred legacy promotion/discount when subtotal + shipping exceeds total.
