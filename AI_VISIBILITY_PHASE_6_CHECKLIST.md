# Peptide Products SEO Phase 6 - AI Visibility and Entity SEO

This phase adds an AI/search-discovery layer on top of the Phase 5 SEO work.

## What changed

- Added `/llms.txt` as a machine-readable summary for AI/search systems.
- Updated `robots.ts` to explicitly allow important search and AI-discovery crawlers while continuing to block private/utility paths such as `/admin`, `/api`, `/cart`, `/checkout`, and `/order-success`.
- Strengthened sitewide `Organization` and `WebSite` structured data with clearer entity details for Peptide Products Ltd.
- Added `/research-peptide-documentation` as a public guide page explaining product pages, available test reports, quality information, shipping/support pathways, and research-use-only restrictions.
- Added the documentation guide to the sitemap.
- Added internal links to the documentation guide from the footer and About page.
